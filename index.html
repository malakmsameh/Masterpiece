Today's advice

Eat a little more color.

Half your plate in vegetables and fruit — that's it. It sounds simple because it is. Bright produce brings fiber, vitamins, and the kind of nourishment that keeps you feeling steady through the day.

Add one new vegetable to tonight's dinner — any color but green.
Keep a bowl of washed fruit on the counter where you'll see it.
Pair it with protein and a whole grain to stay full for longer.
