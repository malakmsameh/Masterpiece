A one-pan chicken and tomato rice dish

Prep 10 min Cook 30 min Makes 2 servings 460 kcal per serving
  1. Dice the onion and tomatoes. Cut chicken into even pieces.
  2. Warm olive oil, soften onion, then add chicken and spices.
  3. Stir in rinsed rice, tomatoes and water. Cover and simmer until rice is tender.
  4. Confirm chicken reaches 74°C / 165°F. Rest, fluff and serve with fresh herbs.
