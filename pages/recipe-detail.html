Chicken Shawarma Bowl

Tender, spiced chicken, fluffy rice and a generous helping of fresh vegetables. All the joy of shawarma, in a beautifully balanced bowl.

Ingredients

Servings: 2

Allergies: dairy (yogurt), sesame (hummus), check labels when using store-bought ingredients.

Make something wonderful

How to make it

1

Marinate the chicken

Mix yogurt, half the olive oil, cumin, paprika, coriander and minced garlic. Coat the chicken well and leave to marinate while you prepare the rice.

2

Cook the rice

Rinse rice until the water runs clear. Add to a pan with 200 ml water and a pinch of salt. Bring to a boil, cover and simmer gently for 12–15 minutes. Rest for 5 minutes.

3

Prepare the fresh vegetables

Dice cucumber and tomatoes. Toss with lemon juice, chopped parsley and the remaining olive oil. Set aside until ready to serve.

4

Sear the chicken

Heat a skillet over medium-high heat. Cook chicken for 6–8 minutes per side, depending on thickness. Check the thickest part reaches 74°C / 165°F. Rest, then slice.

5

Build your bowl

Divide rice between two bowls. Add the vegetables, sliced chicken and a spoonful of hummus. Finish with lemon and parsley, and serve warm.

A little kitchen wisdom

For a lighter portion, use a little less rice and extra vegetables. A smaller serving can be logged separately in Healthy Day—your meal plan remains unchanged.

Keep cooked chicken and rice refrigerated promptly in separate airtight containers. Use within 3 days and reheat thoroughly. Nutrition values are estimates per standard serving.
